## What it is

⭐ A complete testimonial system for WordPress, free and with no cap on testimonials, categories or saved displays. Testimonials live in the plugin's own database tables, not in posts and post meta, so lists, filters and displays stay fast as your collection grows:

- **Testimonials** - star ratings, client photos, role, company and website, plus video testimonials from YouTube, Vimeo or your own files
- **Approval workflow** - pending, approved, rejected and draft; nothing appears on your site until you approve it
- **Four layouts** - grid, list, masonry and slider, organized and filtered with categories
- **Display builder** - colours, typography, photo position, ratings and pagination with a live preview; each display is saved and can be placed on any number of pages
- **Submission form** - `[plugixa_testimonial_form]` lets visitors send a testimonial with a rating and a photo, with optional Google reCAPTCHA
- **Request emails** - ask a customer for a testimonial with a single-use link to the form, their name and email already filled in
- **Import and export** - CSV and JSON, both ways, plus custom fields for extra testimonial data
- **SEO and social proof** - Review and AggregateRating schema (JSON-LD) from your approved testimonials, and social-proof notification popups
- **Integrations** - a Gutenberg block, the `[plugixa_testimonial]` shortcode, an Elementor widget, a WooCommerce product tab, and Polylang and WPML support
- **Roles** - works with your WordPress roles: authors can approve, editors can delete, settings stay with administrators

**Review import** from Google, Facebook and WooCommerce (with an optional daily sync), **PDF export** and **demo data** are part of Plugixa Testimonial Pro. A full tour is on the [documentation](https://www.plugixa.com/documentation/plugixa-testimonial?utm_source=github.com&utm_medium=repo_readme&utm_campaign=plugixa-testimonial&utm_content=docs) - and on the [WordPress.org plugin page](https://wordpress.org/plugins/plugixa-testimonial/).

## Who is it for?

Site owners who want to show what their customers say, and only what they have approved. Businesses that collect testimonials through a form on their site or by emailing a request to a customer. WooCommerce shops that want testimonials on their product pages. Multilingual sites running Polylang or WPML, where each visitor sees the testimonial in their language.

## Get it

The free plugin itself is hosted on WordPress.org - install it from your dashboard (**Plugins → Add New → search "Plugixa Testimonial"**) or fetch it directly:

```
Latest release:  https://downloads.wordpress.org/plugin/plugixa-testimonial.latest-stable.zip
Install by ZIP:  Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin → choose the ZIP → Activate
Requires:        WordPress 6.5+, PHP 8.2+
```
